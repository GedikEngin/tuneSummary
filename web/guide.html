■ Guide · about 2 minutes, then a few days of waiting

Get your data

Spotify keeps your full listening history and gives it to you on request. Here's exactly how to ask for it.

§ 01

Request the export

Works in any browser, phone or computer. Not in the Spotify app.

  1. Open your Spotify privacy settings

    Go to spotify.com/account/privacy ↗ and log in. (From the account page it's under Security and privacy → Account privacy.)

  2. Tick "Extended streaming history"

    Scroll to Download your data. Tick the box for Extended streaming history. That's the one with every play since you made your account, including skips, shuffle and devices.

    You can untick "Account data" and "Technical log information"; TuneSummary doesn't need them. If you only want a quick look, "Account data" alone arrives sooner but covers just the last year with less detail.

  3. Press "Request data"

    Spotify sends you an email titled something like "Confirm your data request". Open it and press Confirm. Nothing happens until you do.

  4. Wait

    Spotify says it can take up to 30 days; it usually takes a few. When it's ready you get an email, "Your Spotify data is ready to download". The download link in it works for 14 days.

  5. Download and upload

    Download my_spotify_data.zip. Don't unzip it. Upload it to TuneSummary →

    Your browser opens the zip and sends only the play history files' contents (track, artist, album, time, length, skip/shuffle, device type, country). Everything else stays on your device.

§ 02

Remind me

Optional · one email, plus one more a week later if you haven't uploaded

Leave your email and we'll create your account and remind you when your export is likely ready, with a link to upload it. You'll get a sign-in link right away; the reminder is set once you click it.

§ 03

Keep it current

Optional · free Last.fm account · about 3 minutes

An export is a snapshot. To see new plays without waiting for another export, let Spotify send each play to Last.fm ↗ (this is called scrobbling) and link that Last.fm profile here. It also works before your export arrives: link Last.fm and you see stats right away, starting from when you connected Spotify to it.

  1. Make a Last.fm account

    Sign up for free at last.fm/join ↗, or sign in if you have one.

  2. Connect Spotify to Last.fm

    On Last.fm open Settings → Applications ↗. Next to Spotify scrobbling, press Connect and log in to Spotify to allow it. From now on every song you play on Spotify (any device) is added to your Last.fm profile.

    Last.fm only records plays from the moment you connect; it can't see older Spotify history. That's what the export is for.

  3. Keep your listening public

    In Settings → Privacy ↗, leave Hide recent listening information unticked. TuneSummary reads your plays through Last.fm's public API and can't see a hidden history.

  4. Link it here

    On your stats page, use the Live updates · Last.fm box to link your profile. We import your recent plays straight away, then keep them up to date. Press ↻ Update my latest stats any time for the newest plays.

Last.fm doesn't record how long you listened, so lengths are estimated from your own export (or the track's length). When you upload a newer export, it replaces the Last.fm plays for the period it covers, so nothing is counted twice. Recent plays powered by AudioScrobbler · Last.fm.
