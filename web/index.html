§ 02

How it works

  1. Ask Spotify for your data

    Spotify lets everyone download their full listening history ("Extended streaming history"). It takes a minute to request. Step-by-step guide →

  2. Wait a few days

    Spotify says up to 30 days; it's usually a few. We can email you a reminder when it's likely ready.

  3. Upload the zip

    Your browser opens my_spotify_data.zip and sends only the list of plays. Upload again later and only new plays are added.

§ 03

Privacy, plainly

We store your email address and your list of plays. That's it: no Spotify password, no Spotify login, no access to your Spotify account. The other files in your export (account details, search history, inferences) never leave your browser.

We don't sell or share your data. There are no third-party trackers or analytics scripts; the server only counts page views. You can download or delete everything from your account page, and deletion is immediate.
